About us

Harborline builds route-planning software for regional shipping companies in Northern Europe. More than 300 vessels use our platform every day to plan port calls and reduce fuel consumption.

In 2024 we released Tidewise, a forecasting engine that cut average fuel use by 12% for our customers. Our backend runs on Go and PostgreSQL, and our forecasting models are written in Python.

We are hiring a senior backend engineer to scale our real-time tracking service, which processes position updates from every vessel on the platform.
